NORTH

The work is already happening up here.

The sessions have a strong focus on AI, alongside creative technology more broadly. Most of the useful knowledge sits with the teams already doing the work, which is what these meet-ups are for.

Each session is built around a practical demonstration from a fellow industry professional and a showcase of work, with time to talk it through afterwards. The aim is that you leave with something you can put into practice.

Our five aims

What we’re actually here to do.

The North of England has a wealth of industry talent. Our aim is to showcase it and help it grow.

Connect the region properly

Studios, broadcasters, agencies, universities and technology partners across the North know each other far less well than they should. Getting the right people in one room, regularly, is the first job.

Share what’s actually working

Real projects with real numbers, explained by the people who did the work, including the parts that went badly. Those tend to be the most useful.

Open up opportunity

Circulating funding routes, joint bids, partnerships and commercial openings so they reach the whole community, not just the usual few.

Raise the region’s profile

The national conversation about creative technology still defaults to London. A visible Northern community changes who gets invited and who gets funded.

Build a route in

Making it easier for people early in their careers, and those moving across from adjacent disciplines, to find their way into this work and the people already doing it.

The first year’s lead theme
AI

AI is moving faster than most teams can keep pace with. It’s the first year’s focus, alongside creative technology more broadly.

Legislation is part of the conversation.

The rules are being written while the work happens. We make room to talk about them, not as a compliance lecture, but as peers comparing how they’re actually handling it.

EU

The AI Act is in force, phasing in since 2025, and its reach doesn’t stop at the Channel. Any studio working with European audiences or clients is already inside it, from transparency and disclosure to how general-purpose models are used.

UK

No equivalent Act yet. A growth-focused bill has been signalled but isn’t law. That leaves creative teams making their own calls on provenance, disclosure, and where the human sits, often ahead of the rulebook.
